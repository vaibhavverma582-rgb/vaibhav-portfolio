My academic path

From geography student to researcher

My interest in geography grew from seeing how patterns on a map can explain what is happening on the ground. I completed my B.A. (Hons) in Geography with First Division and Distinction, and am now pursuing postgraduate study while building a foundation for doctoral research.

Along the way, qualifying UGC-NET in Geography three times strengthened my academic direction. Field surveys, conference presentations and collaborative projects have also shaped how I approach research: stay curious, be precise, and keep the human context in view.
