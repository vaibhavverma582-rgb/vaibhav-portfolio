Geographer by training, geospatial analyst by obsession.

I’m a Geography postgraduate from Uttar Pradesh, India, with a strong academic record and demonstrated research aptitude in geomorphology, remote sensing and environmental systems. My work applies geospatial analysis to land-use & land-cover change, coastal processes and river-basin dynamics.

From mapping three decades of change in the Mahanadi basin with MODIS time-series to listening to riverside communities in Cuttack — I believe the best environmental decisions start with maps that tell the truth.
